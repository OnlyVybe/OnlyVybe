## Featured projects

### [cleanyourtv for KIVI 24H600GR](https://github.com/OnlyVybe/cleanyourtv-kivi-24h600gr)

A toolkit for **KIVI 24H600GR (Android TV 7.1.1)** that combines:

- A reversible debloat procedure with safety checks and recovery support
- A Russian-language, Apple TV-style home launcher adapted for Android 7.1.1
- Python, PowerShell, and ADB utilities
- Setup guides, troubleshooting notes, and verified device-specific information

[Repository](https://github.com/OnlyVybe/cleanyourtv-kivi-24h600gr) · [Documentation](https://github.com/OnlyVybe/cleanyourtv-kivi-24h600gr/tree/main/docs) · [Releases](https://github.com/OnlyVybe/cleanyourtv-kivi-24h600gr/releases)

### [cleanyourtv for Samsung UE32N5300](https://github.com/OnlyVybe/cleanyourtv-samsung-ue32n5300)

A device-specific privacy guide for **Samsung UE32N5300 (Tizen 4.0)**:

- Disable ACR and optional advertising consent settings through the TV menu
- Explain which upstream privacy steps apply to this model and which do not
- Document DNS-level filtering for Smart Hub ads and remaining telemetry
- Provide checklists, verification steps, and rollback instructions

No root, ADB, or software installation on the TV is required. The TV-menu settings are documented as completed; guest-network isolation and router-based DNS filtering remain future steps.

[Repository](https://github.com/OnlyVybe/cleanyourtv-samsung-ue32n5300) · [Documentation](https://github.com/OnlyVybe/cleanyourtv-samsung-ue32n5300/tree/main/docs)

### [cleanyourtv for LG 43LJ595V-ZD](https://github.com/OnlyVybe/cleanyourtv-lg-43lj595v)

A privacy and advertising-control guide for **LG 43LJ595V-ZD (2017, webOS 3.9.3)**:

- Limit advertising tracking and reset the advertising identifier
- Disable promotional recommendations on the home screen
- Reduce background network activity in standby
- Document DNS-level filtering for remaining LG advertising and telemetry domains

The TV-menu settings are documented as completed on the device. Router-based DNS filtering is prepared as a next step. No root, ADB, or software installation on the TV is required.

[Repository](https://github.com/OnlyVybe/cleanyourtv-lg-43lj595v) · [Documentation](https://github.com/OnlyVybe/cleanyourtv-lg-43lj595v/tree/main/docs)
